# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We — contributors and maintainers of CapTracker / Relay — pledge to make participation in
this project a harassment-free, respectful and professional experience for everyone,
regardless of background, role, or level of experience.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the team.
- Showing empathy towards other colleagues.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (repository, pull requests, issues,
reviews) and in any AlphaSights channel where the project is discussed.

## Enforcement

As an internal AlphaSights project, this Code of Conduct is governed by AlphaSights' own
HR and workplace-conduct policies, which take precedence. Concerns may be raised with the
maintainer (sahad.salmi@alphasights.com) or through the normal AlphaSights People/HR
channels.
